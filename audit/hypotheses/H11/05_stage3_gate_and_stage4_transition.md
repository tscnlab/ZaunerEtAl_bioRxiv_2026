Date: 2026-08-06

Status: approved

## Decision

The author explicitly accepted the revised standalone H11 reader-facing report
and authorized the continuing H11 task to create the scientific analysis-
preparation and provenance companion.

The accepted result remains the global biological-sex difference in the
complete near-eye curve, with complementary support at chest. The secondary
two-test BH decomposition limits attribution to an independently resolved
level or shape component; it does not invalidate the global result. The
activity-context analysis remains an exploratory same-sample sensitivity and
does not establish mediation or mechanism.

The companion will be created at
`audit/hypotheses/H11/H11_analysis_preparation.qmd` under REPORT-007. It is a
bounded documentation and provenance page, not a new inferential analysis. It
may verify file identities, schemas, keys, exact samples, and lightweight
descriptive summaries from frozen H11 model frames. It must not fit or refit a
model, re-estimate rho, regenerate predictions, rerun a bootstrap, simulate,
or reinterpret the accepted result.

The H11 task owns its companion source, H11-scoped scripts, tests, figures,
paired source data, manifests, and handoff. The coordinator retains ownership
of `_quarto-nathealth.yml`, shared navigation, central ledgers, and manuscript
integration.

## Reopening condition

Reopen the accepted reader result only if preparation documentation exposes a
substantive discrepancy in an input, model frame, model specification,
estimate, uncertainty interval, diagnostic assessment, sensitivity, or claim.
Wording, provenance, link, or display-only repairs do not reopen the accepted
inference.
