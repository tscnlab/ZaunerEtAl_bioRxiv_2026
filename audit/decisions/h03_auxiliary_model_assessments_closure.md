## Within/between-participant sensitivity

The accepted within/between-participant check is a secondary population-mean
quasi-Tweedie log-link sensitivity with participant-clustered covariance. Its
fixed mean formula is:

```r
geo_medi_1h ~ site + light_source +
  between_source_1 + between_source_2 + between_source_3 +
  between_source_4 + between_source_5 + between_source_6
```

It is not a mixed model, random-intercept model, random-slope model, or
variance-component analysis. Its purpose is to assess the sensitivity of the
population-mean category comparisons to stable between-participant light-source
composition.

The accepted results preserve the overall light-source category conclusion:

- near-eye external light during sleep is 0.333 relative to indoor electric
  light, 95% CI 0.190 to 0.585. The point estimate shifts by 25.8% from the
  primary ratio, retains its direction, and its interval contains the primary
  ratio;
- near-eye outdoor electric light is 1.019, 95% CI 0.336 to 3.090, with
  FDR-adjusted p = 0.973. Its trivial direction change remains null and
  imprecise; and
- chest external light during sleep is 0.244, 95% CI 0.116 to 0.514. The
  point estimate shifts by 34.0% while retaining direction.

These results must not be described as participant-specific slopes, mixed-model
variance, or a participant variance partition.

## Auxiliary participant random-intercept assessment

The new auxiliary model is a `glmmTMB` Tweedie log-link model fitted by maximum
likelihood with fixed variance power 1.539919:

```r
geo_medi_1h ~ site * light_source + (1 | participant)
```

It uses the accepted near-eye frame of 17,935 participant-hours from 140
participants, 801 participant-days, nine sites, and seven light-source
categories. It has no participant-day effect, random light-source slope, or
AR(1) term.

Using Nakagawa's model-based variance decomposition with the lognormal
distribution-specific variance approximation:

- marginal R-squared is 0.79585;
- conditional R-squared is 0.87623;
- the conditional-minus-marginal participant increment is 0.08037;
- adjusted participant ICC is 0.39371; and
- unadjusted participant ICC is 0.08037.

The refit-based hierarchy-respecting Shapley allocation of the full marginal
R-squared is:

| Component | R-squared allocation | Share of full marginal R-squared |
|---|---:|---:|
| Study site | 0.05630 | 7.07% |
| Light source | 0.71045 | 89.27% |
| Study site by light source | 0.02910 | 3.66% |

The five hierarchy-valid nested fits all converged without warnings, had
positive-definite Hessians, and were non-singular. The committed test verifies
the allocation efficiency, exact fitted sample, family, link, power, model
diagnostics, and six-file artifact manifest.

The locally generated auxiliary artifacts are intentionally excluded by the
project artifact ignore rule and are reproducibly generated by the committed R
script. Their controlling local manifest is
`artifacts/12_manifests/H03/H03_near_eye_participant_random_intercept_manifest.csv`,
SHA-256 `81bab5291ca9f9aa24a39cae991857b8f5b39046090da2e4f4c984f4ea7c2306`.

## Interpretation boundary

This auxiliary model is descriptive only. It does not change the accepted
population-mean primary estimand, category tests, multiplicity families,
estimates, or claims. The model lacks participant-day and temporal correlation
terms, retains lag-one Pearson residual correlation 0.288, and gives an
observed exact-zero fraction of 27.8% versus a 39.8% working-model fraction.
Its R-squared values and Shapley allocations are model-based point summaries,
not causal shares of exposure variance, not uncertainty-qualified estimates,
and not interchangeable with the primary quasi-deviance R-squared or temporal
GAM allocations.

No H03 estimate from either auxiliary analysis may be imported into another
hypothesis model, multiplicity family, diagnostic verdict, or claim. Other
hypotheses may link to H03 for context only and must retain their own analysis
contracts and outputs.
