## Approved chat text

### Results in brief

All differences are percentage points (pp), with 95% confidence intervals in parentheses.

- **Main day-type results:** In the any-valid primary sample, equal-site Free-minus-Work differences were **−5.1 pp** (−7.8 to −2.4) for Daytime, **+5.9 pp** (+1.4 to +10.5) for Pre-sleep, and **−6.5 pp** (−9.1 to −3.8) for Sleep.

- **Coverage and temporal limitations:** Requiring at least 80% coverage gave a Pre-sleep difference of **+4.1 pp** (−0.9 to +9.1). This interval includes zero, so the Pre-sleep coverage criterion was not met. Temporal dependence also remains unresolved in the main analysis.

- **Within participants, exploratory:** Per 10 pp higher-than-usual Daytime adherence, adjusted differences were **−0.3 pp** (−1.0 to +0.4) for Sleep and **−1.1 pp** (−2.7 to +0.6) for Pre-sleep. Both intervals include zero. The within-participant association claim remains withheld because temporal dependence could not be adequately addressed.

- **Between participants, exploratory:** Per 10 pp higher observed average Daytime adherence, adjusted differences were **−2.5 pp** (−3.6 to −1.5) for Sleep and **−3.6 pp** (−5.9 to −1.2) for Pre-sleep. Both intervals exclude zero, and both tests met the separate four-effect false-discovery-rate threshold. These remain limited observational associations over the monitoring period.

Neither analysis establishes causation or stable participant types.
