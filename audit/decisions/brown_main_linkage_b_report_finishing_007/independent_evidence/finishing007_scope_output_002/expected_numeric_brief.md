All differences below are percentage points (pp), with 95% confidence intervals in parentheses.

- **Main day-type results:** In the any-valid primary sample, equal-site Free-minus-Work differences were -5.1 pp (-7.8 pp to -2.4 pp) for Daytime, +5.9 pp (+1.4 pp to +10.5 pp) for Pre-sleep, and -6.5 pp (-9.1 pp to -3.8 pp) for Sleep.
- **Coverage and temporal qualification:** Requiring at least 80% coverage gave a Pre-sleep difference of +4.1 pp (-0.9 pp to +9.1 pp). This interval includes zero, so the Pre-sleep coverage criterion was not met. Temporal dependence also remains unresolved in the main analysis.
- **Within participants, exploratory:** Per 10 pp higher-than-usual Daytime adherence, the adjusted differences were -0.3 pp (-1.0 pp to +0.4 pp) for Sleep and -1.1 pp (-2.7 pp to +0.6 pp) for Pre-sleep. Both intervals include zero. The within-participant association claim remains withheld because temporal dependence could not be adequately addressed.
- **Between participants, exploratory:** Per 10 pp higher observed average Daytime adherence, the adjusted differences were -2.5 pp (-3.6 pp to -1.5 pp) for Sleep and -3.6 pp (-5.9 pp to -1.2 pp) for Pre-sleep. Both intervals exclude zero and both tests met the separate four-effect FDR threshold. These are limited observational associations over the monitoring period.

Neither analysis establishes causation or stable participant types.
