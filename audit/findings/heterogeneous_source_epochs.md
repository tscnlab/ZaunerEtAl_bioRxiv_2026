# Heterogeneous source-epoch finding

Finding ID: `IMP-020`  
Status: repair approved; clean alignment rerun pending  
Date: 2026-07-30

## Finding

The pinned light releases are not uniformly sampled at 10 seconds for every
participant stream. R 4.6.1 verification of all 17 placement/site files found
unique absolute timestamp keys and no negative light values, but two
participant-level epoch exceptions:

| Site and participant | Placement | Verified epoch | One-minute intervals |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `FUSPCEU_S014` | near-eye | 60 seconds | 10,080 |
| `RISE_S011` | near-eye | 1 second | 2,880 |

All other inspected participant streams use a 10-second epoch. For the two
exceptions, every affected minute has exactly the expected one or 60 source
rows, respectively. A universal six-subepoch rule would therefore discard
the complete FUSPCEU stream and reject the RISE stream as overfull even
though both are regular, supported measurements.

## Approved repair

Infer and validate one dominant regular epoch for each
site-participant-placement stream on the true UTC axis. The epoch must divide
60 seconds exactly. A primary one-minute value requires the complete expected
number of finite subepochs for that stream:

- 60 of 60 for a 1-second stream;
- 6 of 6 for a 10-second stream; and
- 1 of 1 for a 60-second stream.

The stream epoch, expected subepochs, observed subepochs, finite subepochs,
and completeness flag remain in the minute artifact. No participant is
excluded merely because their valid source epoch differs from 10 seconds.
This preserves the current arithmetic aggregation estimand while removing an
incorrect new hard-coded assumption.

## Partial-minute scope

Across all pinned streams, 3,436 minutes contain at least one but fewer than
the expected number of finite MEDI subepochs. Requiring full finite support
leaves 3,041 of these minutes missing that would become finite under a
50%-within-minute rule. Most occur at KNUST. The primary analysis retains the
complete-subepoch rule; a relaxed rule is eligible only as a prespecified
one-axis sensitivity if downstream reconciliation shows it is material.

## Other source checks

- No raw stream contains a duplicated participant/absolute-timestamp key.
- No finite MEDI or LIGHT value is negative.
- No raw or one-minute MEDI value is exactly 100,000 lx.
- Before state or coverage filtering, 126 complete one-minute MEDI means are
  at or above 100,000 lx and will be masked by the approved operating-range
  rule.

The full stream-level and partial-minute tables are generated under
`artifacts/01_imported/` and will be regenerated by Preparation 01.
