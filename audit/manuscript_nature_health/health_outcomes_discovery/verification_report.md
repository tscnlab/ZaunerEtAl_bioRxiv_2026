## Strongest conclusion

Objective personal light is associated with multiple health-relevant outcomes at population scale, but existing large health cohorts do not supply the harmonised international near-eye melanopic baseline developed by this manuscript. The manuscript can use the epidemiology to motivate exposure assessment and use the Brown contexts to describe measured exposure. It cannot claim causality, individual health risk, clinical adherence or a first or largest study.

## Limitations

This was a bounded manuscript update rather than a systematic review. Subscription indexes were not searched; citation chaining was best effort; one selected article was screened at abstract level; there was no dual independent screening or formal risk-of-bias assessment. Exact-title status searches may miss poorly indexed notices.
