# Health-outcome evidence discovery search log

Discovery mode: live, verification-first, bounded literature discovery  
Search date: 2026-08-14, Europe/Berlin  
Decision: repair the Nature Health Introduction and Discussion so that the health relevance of personal light exposure is supported without treating wrist or bedside measurements as ocular dose, and without converting observational associations into causal claims.

## Frozen brief

- Population and context: community-dwelling adults under everyday conditions.
- Construct: objectively measured personal light exposure, including day, evening, night, timing and 24-hour pattern.
- Measurement chain: wearable wrist, near-eye or bedside sensors. Placement had to be extractable.
- Outcomes: sleep timing or sleepiness, psychiatric outcomes, obesity or metabolic outcomes, cardiovascular disease and mortality.
- Designs: observational cross-sectional and longitudinal cohorts. The Brown expert recommendations and one large descriptive exposure study were retained as contextual records.
- Exclusions: satellite-only exposure, occupational shift-work inference without personal light measurement, laboratory-only exposure, animal studies and non-objective exposure measures.
- Bounds: English-language journal records available by 2026-08-14. No lower date limit. Preprints, corrections and retractions were in scope for version and status checks.
- This is a focused manuscript update, not a systematic review. Scopus, Web of Science, Embase and other subscription databases were not searched.

## Search limitations

The search was deliberately bounded around the health claims needed for this manuscript. It did not perform dual screening, a registered protocol, formal risk-of-bias assessment or certainty grading. Exact-title status searches can miss notices that are not indexed or use different titles. The findings support citation selection and calibrated framing, not a claim of review completeness.
