## Manuscript disposition

The verified committee names and approval references were added to paragraph P-M03. Dortmund (DE) is explicitly described as operating under the TUM multicentre approval `2024-118-S-SB`, consistent with the author's statement that BAuA has no separate approval file. Delft (NL) is described as having a positive committee review dated 29 May 2024, with no reference number stated in the supplied letter.

No blanket Declaration of Helsinki sentence was added because that statement was not uniformly documented by the supplied approval letters. Ethics approvals do not establish engagement in study-question selection, design, interpretation, governance or dissemination. The author subsequently confirmed on 2026-08-14 that no participant or community co-design occurred, and the manuscript now states that fact directly.
