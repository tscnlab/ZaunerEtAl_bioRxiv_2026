## Author clarification

The author clarified that the participants in the protocol-development trial are the participants recruited at the Tübingen site and form the Tübingen cohort in the present analysis. They are not a separate pilot sample. Participant feedback from this initial site informed procedural refinements before implementation at the remaining sites.

The author approved removing the blanket negative co-design statement and replacing it with a concise factual description linked to the published protocol. The manuscript does not call this formal participant or community co-design.
