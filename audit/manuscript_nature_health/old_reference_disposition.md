# Disposition of the 98 references in the Nature Medicine manuscript

The revised Nature Health manuscript now cites 90 unique sources. Of the 98 numbered references in the Nature Medicine manuscript, 83 are retained. Seven current sources are new: four statistical-method sources needed by the accepted analyses, one recent two-cohort exposure study, one cardiovascular-outcome study and the public wearing-position preprint. The earlier Phase 3 draft retained only 44 of the old references, so this revision restores 39 old sources whose context, method, software or data-provenance roles remain relevant.

Fifteen old references remain dropped. They were removed because their claim is no longer made, their construct does not match the measured exposure, they duplicate a retained source, or the cited method or software was not used. They were not removed merely to shorten the bibliography.

## Dropped references and reasons
