## 9. Phase 1 source-use boundary

- Legacy prose can be retained only where it remains scientifically accurate and fits the new argument.
- Accepted result sources, companions, later decisions, and handoffs control scientific claims.
- Near-eye is the primary ocular-exposure measure. Chest is separate complementary evidence on paired or otherwise explicitly described samples.
- The chest option's recruitment-feasibility role is an author-confirmed implementation fact and must remain distinct from analytical eligibility and paired inference.
- The public placement preprint should be cited for structured differences across measurement positions. Its overlapping MeLiDos data require an explicit related-work statement, and its results cannot be presented as independent replication.
- Sleep-period sensor values describe the bedside sleep environment.
- Hourly evidence is the main day-context analysis. Daily evidence is complementary.
- Brown recommendation comparisons are fractions of valid measured minutes in contextual ranges, not participant classifications or health outcomes.
- Provisional display roles must not be converted into fixed manuscript numbering before REPORT-017 and author visual approval close.
- The manuscript will not claim health effects, causal determinants, placement equivalence, a universal chest correction, or recommendation adherence.
