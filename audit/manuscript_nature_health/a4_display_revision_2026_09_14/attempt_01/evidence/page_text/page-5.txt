 88 A multisite baseline and recommendation adherence

 89 The normalised roster comprised 191 participants across Borås (SE), Delft (NL), Dortmund
 90 (DE), Tübingen (DE), Munich (DE), Madrid (ES), Izmir (TR), San José (CR) and Kumasi
 91 (GH). Of these, 184 contributed 1,478 participant-days with recorded, non-all-zero light data
 92 at either position before quality screening. After screening, the near-eye dataset contained
 93 141 participants and 816 participant-days, the complementary chest dataset 154 and 902,
 94 and the common sample 112 participants and 643 participant-days. Near-eye
 95 measurements provide ocular-exposure evidence during wear, whereas chest
 96 measurements are non-ocular; both describe the bedside sleep environment during reported
 97 sleep. Supplementary Table S1 gives the sample flow.

 98 The pooled near-eye record followed the expected 24-hour rhythm: low overnight exposure,
 99 a morning rise, higher daytime values and an evening decline. Seventeen complementary
100 metrics captured thematic streams rather than one score. Level metrics described daily
101 geometric mean melanopic EDI and the brightest and darkest supported 10-hour means;
102 duration and timing metrics quantified exposure ranges, sustained bright periods, bright or
103 dark windows and threshold crossings. Dose integrated intensity, regularity captured stability
104 and fragmentation, and melanopic daylight efficacy (MDER) described spectral composition
105 relative to visual illuminance. These features are not interchangeable health-response
106 estimates. Supplementary Table S2 and Supplementary Figure S1 give the metric dictionary
107 and site distributions; Supplementary Figure S2 shows a worked derivation. Figure 1 and
108 Table 1 summarise the design and data structure.
