618 Time, sleep/wake status and coverage preparation

619 Processing preserved both actual time in Coordinated Universal Time and local wall-clock
620 time. Actual time ordered observations and measured elapsed gaps; local time aligned daily
621 patterns across sites. Repeated local minutes during autumn clock changes remained
622 distinct in actual time, and structurally absent spring minutes remained missing. Melanopic
623 EDI values at or above the validated 100,000-lx operating boundary were unavailable
624 analytically but retained in the raw channel with a flag.

625 Diary-defined sleep began at reported sleep preparation and ended at reported wake. Sleep
626 values were retained as the intended bedside record. Declared waking non-wear made the
627 analytical light value unavailable; missing wear labels were not treated as non-wear.
628 Complete 1,440-minute local participant-day grids preserved unsupported periods as
629 missing rather than deleting rows or replacing them with zero. A day was eligible when at
630 least 80% of this combined waking and bedside-sleep record had valid melanopic EDI.
631 Hourly summaries required at least 50% valid minutes in that hour, but an unsupported hour
632 did not erase valid minutes from the daily decision or minute-based metrics. Two otherwise
633 eligible near-eye days and three chest days with finite but entirely zero melanopic EDI were
634 excluded by the defined exact-all-zero screen.
