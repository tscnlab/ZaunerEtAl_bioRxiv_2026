222 Site associations were stream-specific. Site differences met the FDR criterion for 8 of 17
223 near-eye metrics: all three level metrics, waking time above 250 lx, the brightest- and
224 darkest-10-hour midpoints, and the mean and last timing above 250 lx. They were not FDR-
225 retained for dose, spectral composition, stability, fragmentation, sustained bright exposure,

228 participant-day metrics. Among the eight metrics with site support, site-associated part R²

230 contribution in the daily-pattern analysis.
