748 equally spaced photoperiod values with 95% intervals79. A qualifying descriptive transition
749 required the preceding slope interval to be wholly above zero, the next interval to include
750 zero and every later interval through the recorded maximum to include zero as well. This
751 procedure located a change in the fitted slope but did not test equivalence, a mechanism or
752 a physiological ceiling. Expanded-basis, fixed-site, common-sample and leave-one-site-out
753 analyses assessed stability. The gap-timing-unaware sensitivity dataset still passed the
754 50%-per-hour and 80%-per-day rules, but did not use the timing of remaining missing
755 observations for an additional metric-specific adjustment.

756 Immediate-context and routine analyses

757 Light-source and activity-environment analyses modelled the zero-aware one-hour
758 geometric mean melanopic EDI with fixed-power quasi-Tweedie log-mean models and
759 participant-cluster-robust inference73,74. Additive fixed-site models supplied primary category
760 tests. Category-by-site interaction models supplied site-average category estimates, ratios
761 and site-specific context when the interaction was supported. The activity-environment
762 analysis retained fractional 1/k weights for concurrent labels. FDR adjustments were applied
763 to the declared category and site-contrast families. Exact-zero calibration, residual
764 dependence, sparse cells and deletion refits were retained as model checks. A secondary
765 population-mean sensitivity added six participant-level category-proportion terms to the light-
766 source model while retaining participant-clustered covariance. This separated hour-to-hour
767 category differences within participants from between-participant differences in category
768 composition; it did not estimate participant random slopes. Exploratory glmmTMB Tweedie
769 log-link models fitted by maximum likelihood then added one common participant intercept to
770 site-by-category fixed effects at the near-eye position for light source and separately at both
771 positions for the activity-environment variable, with the power fixed at 1.53991976,77. The
772 activity-environment models retained the five named categories, excluded Other-only hours
773 and used exact 1/k weights. Their fixed-predictor variance used the same fractional weights
774 so every participant-hour contributed one unit. Marginal and conditional R² used a lognormal
775 distribution-specific variance approximation, and hierarchy-respecting Shapley allocations
776 distributed marginal R² across site, category and their interaction. Percentages from the
777 exploratory time-of-day models instead partition variance among fitted linear-predictor
778 values. Because they do not compare this with observed outcome variance, they are not R².
779 Results describe them as variation in fitted hourly patterns, not variance explained in raw
780 exposure. These were model-based point descriptions without bootstrap intervals. No model
781 included random category slopes.
