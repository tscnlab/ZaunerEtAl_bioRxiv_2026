469 by biological sex, but immediate-setting-complete analyses did not retain this separation.
470 Person-level patterns may concern specific exposure dimensions rather than a general high-
471 versus-low phenotype.

472 Near-eye sensing approximates the incident ocular field during wake but is not a retinal
473 measurement; chest sensing samples a related environmental field. Studies show structured
474 differences by body position, morphology, posture and surrounding light29–31. The matched
475 sample and parallel placement study show metric- and context-specific MeLiDos differences
476 without a universal correction33. The chest option broadened recruitment among people
477 unwilling to wear glasses-mounted sensors, especially in San José (CR). Study design must
478 balance biological proximity, burden and recruitment while keeping estimands position-
479 specific.

480 The harmonised protocol combined repeated near-eye sensing with local-clock and
481 contextual information across nine sites and seven countries. A generalised additive model
482 separated the shared 24-hour pattern from site, participant and participant-day layers
483 obscured by daily summaries. Collecting these data demanded substantial effort from
484 participants and study teams. Glasses-mounted loggers required fitting, explanation and
485 daily handling; removal, occlusion, charging, discomfort, weather and social acceptability all
486 affected measurement quality41–43. Despite this burden, the protocol measured closer to the
487 incident ocular field at high temporal and contextual resolution.

488 Several limitations qualify these findings. Modest site samples limit site estimates,
489 interactions and subgroup evaluation. Healthy working-age adults may not represent
490 children, adolescents, older or clinical populations, shift workers, people outside
491 employment, or those with restricted mobility or atypical living conditions. Sparse latitude-by-
492 photoperiod coverage leaves latitude, seasonality, culture, climate and built environment
493 entangled and requires broader seasonal sampling. Retrospective hourly setting and light-
494 source reports enabled harmonisation but could not distinguish fine-grained settings or
495 behaviours within home, work or transport. Very low readings require caution. The
496 manufacturer specifies an operating range starting at 1 lx, and independent laboratory
497 testing began at 2 lx44. A scene-based field validation found high overall agreement with
498 laboratory-grade spectral references, but underestimation increased at lower intensities and
499 varied with lighting, time, scene complexity and site45. Single-digit values therefore
500 approximate low-light exposure rather than provide precise measurements. Finally, no health
501 outcome was measured.
