564 Personal light measurements

565 Personal light was recorded every 10 s with spectrally sensitive ActLumus loggers (Condor
566 Instruments, São Paulo, Brazil). Eight visible-band channels were processed by the device
567 to generate photopic and alpha-opic quantities, including melanopic EDI6. The manufacturer
568 specified a factory-calibrated operating range of 1 to 100,000 lx and 10% precision at 1,000
569 lx. Independent testing against a criterion spectrometer across indoor LED and outdoor
570 daylight conditions covered 2 to 100,000 lx and reported mean photopic error of +3.3% (SD
571 13.4%) for ActLumus44. A subsequent scene-based field validation found high overall
572 agreement with laboratory-grade spectral reference instruments, together with systematic
573 underestimation that varied with intensity and setting45. Participants wore the near-eye
574 device centrally on non-prescription spectacle frames and, where available, a
575 complementary pendant-mounted device at the chest. The near-eye position measures light
576 closer to the eye but is not a direct retinal measurement. The chest position provided a
577 separate measurement of the local environmental light field. Paired common-sample
