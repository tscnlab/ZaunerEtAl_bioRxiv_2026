848 Data availability

849 All data used in this study are available through the MeLiDos Project GitHub organisation
850 and are archived in the MeLiDos Zenodo community. Public site datasets have persistent
851 records for Kumasi (GH), Madrid (ES), Dortmund (DE), Izmir (TR), Tübingen (DE), San José
852 (CR), Munich (DE), Borås (SE) and Delft (NL)83–91. These records and other project
853 resources are indexed through the MeLiDos Project Data Hub92 and accessed reproducibly
854 through the documented melidosData interface93.
