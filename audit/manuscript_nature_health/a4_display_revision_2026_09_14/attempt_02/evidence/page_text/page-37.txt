715 Daily temporal architecture

716 The daily-pattern analysis used supported 30-minute arithmetic means, retaining a bin when
717 at least 15 one-minute values were valid. The outcome was log10(melanopic EDI + 0.1 lx). A
718 generalised additive model fitted with mgcv::bam() and fREML combined a cyclic common
719 local-clock smooth, sum-to-zero site curves, participant curves and participant-day random
720 intercepts67–70. A boundary-aware first-order autoregressive working structure prevented
721 residual sequences from crossing participant-day, clock-repeat or gap boundaries. Fitted-
722 curve dispersion was compared on a common 48-bin grid. A Shapley decomposition
723 averaged each component’s addition to R² across all component orders, thereby distributing
724 the model’s explained variance71,72. The decomposition partitioned the full model’s row-
725 weighted in-sample R² for log10(melanopic EDI + 0.1 lx), defined as the reduction in squared
726 prediction error relative to predicting the fitted-sample mean. The percentages therefore
727 partition full-model R² on that transformed response scale. They are not percentages of
728 variance explained in raw melanopic EDI and do not indicate causal or out-of-sample
729 predictive importance. Confidence intervals for dispersion and R²-share ratios used 2,000
730 hierarchical cluster resamples while holding the fitted model structure fixed.

731 Geographic and photoperiod analyses

732 We analysed 17 participant-level or participant-day metrics with metric-specific Gaussian or
733 Tweedie exponential-dispersion models73,74. We treated overall site differences, civil
734 photoperiod (day length including civil twilight), absolute latitude and the adequacy of a
735 linear latitude term relative to site as four separate model questions, each adjusted as a
736 complete 17-test FDR family. Participant-day models included participant random intercepts.
737 Mixed-effects models were fitted with lme4 or glmmTMB, and component R² values were
738 obtained with performance where reported75–77. Site and latitude were not entered as
739 independent fixed predictors in one model because each site has one latitude; site and
740 latitude models instead used the same observations and photoperiod adjustment. Site
741 follow-up contrasts were calculated with emmeans only after a supported overall site test and
742 compared each site with a site-average estimate, obtained by giving each included site
743 equal weight78.
