681 effect-minus-average contrasts. The last family’s coverage repetition assessed stability
682 without another FDR adjustment. Variance decomposition used balanced 54-cell global and
683 18-cell within-window references; Shapley allocations and R² were point-only. Chest models
684 used a separate two-window, eight-site sample and did not test paired placement
685 differences.

686 The exploratory cross-window extension used daytime adherence as the predictor and the
687 preceding sleep or following pre-sleep adherence as outcomes under the same wake-start-
688 date alignment. It retained pairwise-complete observations, not necessarily complete three-
689 window cycles: 1,376 outcome rows comprised 758 daytime-sleep and 618 daytime-pre-
690 sleep pairs from 140 participants and 761 cycles. Its frozen eligibility rules did not include
691 every potential main-model pair. The model separated each participant’s cycle-specific
692 daytime deviation from their observed average, adjusting for window, site, day type, their
693 corresponding interactions and the participant’s day-type composition. Response-scale
694 contrasts used equal-site and 50:50 work/free weighting. The four within- and between-
695 participant associations formed a separate FDR family. Positive serial dependence remained
696 unresolved and some sensitivity fits were non-estimable, so no within-participant day-level
697 claim was stated. Descriptive profiles used unweighted equal-cycle participant means, with
698 daytime deduplicated within participant and cycle, for 139 complete profiles and 417
699 participant-window points. These are anonymous monitoring-period summaries, not ranks or
700 stable participant types.

701 The main model passed all five applicable endpoint-calibration checks in both samples, but
702 temporal dependence still qualified inference. The 80% coverage restriction retained the
703 daytime and sleep directions and interval conclusions; pre-sleep retained direction but not
704 exclusion of zero. Diary-indexed grouping was a separate sensitivity. Pre-sleep interval
705 conclusions also changed under 70% and 90% coverage and restriction to participants
706 observed on both day types. Actual-date temporal endpoint models failed structural checks.
707 Their beta-binomial fallbacks changed both the response family and random-effects
708 structure, retained boundary cautions and failed all five endpoint-calibration checks in both
709 samples. They did not replace the primary model or resolve its temporal limitation. All nine
710 site-deletion checks and four participant-deletion checks were estimable; one participant
711 deletion and the ordinary-binomial diagnostic failed and supplied no derived inference. The
712 separate chest model retained calibration and temporal qualifications. In the exploratory
713 extension, the 80% restriction preserved all four directions and interval conclusions, but
714 incomplete influence checks and unresolved dependence limited robustness claims.
