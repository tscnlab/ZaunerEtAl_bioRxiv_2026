1426 Measurement position, robustness, and reproducibility

1427 Near-eye and complementary chest measurements remain separate sensor-position
1428 estimands. Common-sample analyses use the same participants and participant-days at
1429 both positions and fit the positions separately. They do not pool positions, test equivalence,
1430 or establish a universal correction. Detailed model checks, sensitivities, preregistration
1431 deviations, exact formulas, package versions, and source documentation are provided in the
