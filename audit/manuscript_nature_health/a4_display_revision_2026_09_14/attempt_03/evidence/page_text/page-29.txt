514 Methods

515 Study design and sites

516 MeLiDos is a prospective multicentre observational field study of personal light exposure
517 conducted with a harmonised protocol35. Participants completed an eight-day ambulatory
518 protocol comprising continuous light logging, repeated smartphone assessments and daily
519 diaries under free-living conditions. At an in-person registration visit, participants provided
520 consent, completed baseline questionnaires, received study instructions and were fitted with
521 the loggers. Seven consecutive recording days separated registration and return visits; most
522 sites recorded from Monday to Monday, while Dortmund (DE) recorded from Tuesday to
523 Tuesday. If a return visit was delayed, participants continued until formal study termination
524 and device retrieval. Data were collected between August 2023 and October 2025 at Borås
525 (SE), Delft (NL), Dortmund (DE), Tübingen (DE), Munich (DE), Madrid (ES), Izmir (TR), San
526 José (CR) and Kumasi (GH). The Kumasi (GH) and Izmir (TR) collections are described in
527 site-specific data notes46,47. Sites were an opportunity sample of consortium partners and
528 were not selected to represent national populations. Questionnaire materials were
529 administered in the local language except in Tübingen (DE) and Kumasi (GH), where
530 English was used, and translated materials followed a harmonised translation, review,
531 adjudication, pretesting and documentation process48.

532 Participants, recruitment and ethics

533 The registered target population comprised adults aged 18 to 65 years in full-time
534 employment or part-time employment above 80%. Exclusion criteria included diagnosed
535 psychiatric or sleep disorders, shift work in the preceding two months, regular tobacco or
536 recreational drug use, photosensitising medication, visual impairment incompatible with the
537 devices and residence outside the local study area during recording. The retained near-eye
538 sample included nine participants outside the registered age or employment criteria: one
539 participant older than 65 years, two recorded as not employed and seven recorded as
540 marginally employed; one participant met both the age and employment criteria for
541 exclusion. As a near-eye-only eligibility sensitivity, we refitted the additive and predictor-by-
542 site models after excluding participants recorded as not employed or marginally employed.
543 Students and trainees were retained, and age above 65 years was not an exclusion criterion
544 for this sensitivity. The target was at least 15 participants per site, based on prior power
545 calculations and allowing for up to one-third dropout or incomplete recording49. Participants
546 were recruited through local advertisements and institutional mailing lists. At some sites,
