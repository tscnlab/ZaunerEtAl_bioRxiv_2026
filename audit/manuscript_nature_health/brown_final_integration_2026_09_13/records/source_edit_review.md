## Scope and meaning

- The accepted final main Brown analysis replaces the superseded main sample,
  contrasts, interaction tests and variance summaries. Its chronology is
  explicit: preceding sleep, daytime and following pre-sleep, all labelled by
  the wake-start date. Individual windows qualify independently.
- Pre-sleep no longer carries the previous unconditional robustness statement.
  Its interval includes zero at 80% coverage. Temporal dependence remains
  unresolved. Methods distinguish unsuccessful temporal alternatives from the
  primary model and retain the remaining diagnostic qualifications.
- The cross-window extension remains separate and exploratory. Its four
  estimates are unchanged; no within-participant claim, evidence of absence,
  causal explanation or stable participant type is asserted.
- Main variance summaries use the accepted design-standardized response-scale
  R² quantities. The two reported contribution ratios are arithmetic from the
  frozen contributions, verified in R, not new model decomposition.
- The six main adherence levels are present with their intervals in exact
  Table 2A. They are not repeated in Results prose. This removes duplication,
  not evidence. Main free-versus-work contrasts and site-reference departures
  remain numerical in the prose.
- Non-Brown text, title, abstract, citations, declarations and source structure
  are unchanged from the current live preimage. Full reverse reconstruction
  verifies this; the non-Brown prefixes and suffixes within the two mixed
  paragraphs are checked separately.
