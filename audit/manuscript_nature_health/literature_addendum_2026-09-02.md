# Literature addendum: exposome framing and ActLumus field validation

Date searched: 2026-09-02, Europe/Berlin  
Mode: live, verification-first, bounded update to the existing Nature Health literature evidence base  
Decision: support one concise exposome and non-communicable-disease bridge and update the ActLumus measurement limitation without treating visual patterns as tested intensity cut-points.

## Claim calibration

- The defensible exposome bridge is that mental, metabolic, and cardiovascular outcomes fall within the broad burden of non-communicable disease, for which the exposome provides a framework for studying multiple non-genetic environmental contributions. The manuscript does not claim that personal light is already comprehensively represented in exposome studies.
- The defensible gap is that time-resolved ocular melanopic light remains largely absent from population exposome research. This is narrower than saying that light has never been studied within exposome research.
- The ActLumus preprint reports high overall agreement with laboratory-grade spectral reference instruments across 433 scenes, together with systematic underestimation whose magnitude depended on lighting condition, scene complexity, time of day, and site. Its narrative supports greater caution at low intensities. It does not report inferentially estimated break-points at 10 lx or 1 lx, so those values should not be presented as validated cut-points.

## Verification result

Verified within the stated bounds. The three canonical records have unique citation keys in `references_verified_additions.bib`. No correction or retraction was identified in the inspected sources as of the search date. The arXiv record remains a preprint.
