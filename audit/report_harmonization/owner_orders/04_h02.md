## Approved vocabulary and glossary content

- Define **melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance (melEDI)** at first use. State that the near-eye sensor position is primary but not a direct retinal measurement, and that the chest sensor position is complementary and not ocular exposure.
- Define **participant-day** when its denominator first matters.
- Describe the model first as a **nonlinear GAM analysis**: a generalized additive model (GAM) allows the association with local clock time to bend across the day rather than follow a straight line. `GAM` may remain after this; use `GAMM` only where the mixed-model distinction is technically necessary and explained.
- Explain the transformed scale as `log10(melEDI + 0.1 lx)` and say that a **back-transformed** curve returns to melEDI in lux before using that shorter term.
- Explain each **random effect/smooth** by the remaining variation it represents among participants or participant-days after the reported time/site pattern is considered.
- Explain **autocorrelation** before `AR(1)`: adjacent 30-minute residuals can remain more alike than residuals farther apart, and the AR(1) structure represents this correlation as strongest for adjacent observations and weaker at longer lags. `AR(1)` may remain thereafter.
- Define a **pointwise 95% confidence interval (95% CI)** as uncertainty at one displayed clock time; state that it is not a simultaneous band for the full day and does not multiplicity-control every red segment. `95% CI` may remain later.
- Introduce **false-discovery-rate (FDR) adjustment** at first use and `FDR` thereafter. Replace every reader-facing `BH` abbreviation in prose, captions, alt text, table labels, and notes with FDR wording. The full Benjamini–Hochberg method name may remain only in technical reproducibility detail. Preserve the one-test family, p-value, and decision exactly.
- Define **site-average curve/estimate** as an average across sites that gives each site equal weight. Replace unexplained `equal-site`/`equally weighted` shorthand where this clearer wording applies; technical field names may remain.
- Explain **Shapley allocation** as the average contribution of a model component across all orders in which components could be added, allocating shared fitted-model information rather than counting it repeatedly. Preserve the qualification that this is conditional in-sample model-fit relevance, not causal or cross-validated importance.
- Use **model checks** as the umbrella term; `model diagnostics` may appear once in parentheses or in technical detail. Explain `concurvity` in reader prose as overlap among nonlinear model terms that makes their separate contributions harder to distinguish.
- Explain that the matched comparison uses **the same participants, participant-days, and 30-minute observations at both sensor positions** before using `matched sample` or technical `paired-placement` language. Preserve the no-equivalence/no-direct-placement-effect qualification.
- Define each **sensitivity analysis** by the exact changed dataset, cyclic basis, sample, or placement.
- Replace “remaining heterogeneity is not mainly geographic” with scientifically equivalent plain wording about remaining fitted variation among people/days versus sites.
- Every reader-facing site name must carry its ISO alpha-2 country code in prose, tables, figures, legends, captions, and alt text, using `config/site_display_registry.csv` exactly. Internal site codes may remain only in code/provenance.

Keep the introductory glossary short: include only the cluster of specialized concepts a reader needs before the first principal display, and retain detailed definitions in the relevant methods paragraphs.
