#### Supplementary Figure S17 and Table S15. H11 daily curves and global tests
Figure caption. Near-eye biological-sex-specific fitted melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance curves and Female-to-Male shifted-value ratio. Confidence intervals apply at the displayed clock times. Ribbons and contrast intervals are participant-cluster-robust pointwise 95% confidence intervals, not simultaneous bands, and do not identify a significant clock-time period. Grey shading gives site-average civil-night context with each site weighted equally and is not a model covariate. Biological sex, coded Female or Male, was analysed; gender was recorded separately and was not analysed.
