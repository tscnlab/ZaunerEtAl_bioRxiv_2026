#### Supplementary Figure S5. Site-specific adherence and day-type differences
Caption. Site-specific adherence to the Brown et al. recommendations. A, Modelled Work-day recommendation adherence for each site and Brown recommendation window. Points show site estimates and bars show 95% confidence intervals. The long-dashed blue line gives the equally weighted site-average estimate for each window. Filled diamonds identify stored site-minus-average contrasts that passed their prespecified false-discovery-rate threshold. B, Modelled Free-day minus Work-day differences. The dotted black line marks no difference, and the long-dashed blue line gives the equally weighted site-average difference for each window. Orange diamonds identify contrasts versus zero that passed their prespecified false-discovery-rate threshold. Black asterisks identify departures from the site-average difference that passed the separate prespecified threshold. The rows represent the Daytime, Pre-sleep, and Sleep recommendation windows defined by Brown et al.; Pre-sleep is the three hours before sleep. Sleep-window estimates describe recorded near-eye illuminance during sleep and should not be interpreted as the bedroom environment. All estimates come from pooled interaction models. Sites are not independent replications, and the figure does not estimate causal effects of location.
